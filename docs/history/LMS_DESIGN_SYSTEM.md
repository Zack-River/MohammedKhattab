# Ta3llam design foundation

Landing entrance: the hero heading, supporting copy, action row, microcopy and supplied intro video (or optional course preview) reveal in reading order using the shared 500ms entrance and 80ms stagger tokens. The page reveal scope owns this group; do not add nested reveal scopes to individual hero children. Reduced motion leaves every element immediately visible.

Uses the approved Mohammed Khattab / workshop identity: #fc3522 actions, #101317 canvas, alternating dark surfaces, IBM Plex Sans Arabic and RTL. `../packages/design-system/styles/theme.css` owns shared measurements/colors; `styles/theme.css` is the LMS entry point with product deltas; `styles/workshop.css` imports approved shared visual patterns; styles/lms.css adapts them to LMS screens. Shared primitives and instructor/FAQ/transformation sections are reused from the approved project. New routes use focused components, centralized content and typed contracts. Keep familiar student flows, native accessibility and reduced-motion support.

Course cards show only the real title above a clean cover, then lesson count and duration on the right and the price on the left. The red action sits below this row. Title and cover link to course details; there are no cover tags, repeated subtitles, price captions or separate details links. Enrolled cards additionally show progress and a continue action. Mock prices live in `data/offers.ts`; details keep their demo pricing explanation.

Content scope: montage, graphic design and motion graphics are the core teaching areas. Freelance courses and personal coaching support learners. Do not add content-creation courses or position the platform as teaching content creation. Coaching is presented separately from prerecorded courses.

Use descriptive course names that identify the skill and software, rather than promotional slogans. Hero previews, cards and details share the title from the course record. Course-title hover and keyboard focus change text to the primary red without an underline.

Readability: body copy and field labels use 16px; metadata, lesson statuses and helper text use the 14px caption role. Form inputs and compact controls use 16px. Main red actions use white `text-on-action` labels at 20px/700, exceeding the bold large-text size threshold and retaining the approved white-on-red identity. Compact navigation/search actions use dark `text-on-compact-action` labels to meet the normal-text threshold. The primary fill remains stable on hover. Small brand-accent text on dark cards uses `text-accent-readable`; large course-title hover remains primary red. Lesson titles use 16px/500 and secondary text (primary text when active); statuses use 14px/400 secondary text, overriding the bold numeral style. All readability roles live in `styles/theme.css`.

Color identity: preserve the approved red highlights, course-card top borders, icon accents, tinted supporting panels, selected-state treatments, hero highlight and learning-step hover effects. Small accent text on dark surfaces uses the readable brand tint from `styles/theme.css`. Keep the #fc3522 brand, semantic completion/success green and readable button/status typography. Do not neutralize these brand accents across the LMS; the client explicitly requested their restoration. Introductory-pill removal is independent of the red identity.

Section introductions: omit generic introductory badges that repeat the heading or body. Shared section headings accept an optional context/eyebrow and render it as plain supporting text when it adds information (for example coaching, course category or demo-account context). Landing hero, course showcase, learning steps, instructor, FAQ and final CTA lead with their headings. Catalog, library, resources and certificates omit generic introductory slogans. Preserve personalized greetings, demo indicators, lesson status, selection states and existing section padding. Do not insert blank badge placeholders or hide unwanted labels with CSS.

Motion: all LMS interaction and scroll-reveal timing, easing, distance and stagger values live in `styles/theme.css`. Controls transition explicit color/background/border properties, while existing card/button movement uses the shared surface duration. Main actions have a small press response; play controls scale gently. FAQ answers animate both expansion and collapse using a grid track, with collapsed content inert and aria-hidden. Scroll reveals read inherited theme tokens on the client and keep observer cleanup and preference-change cancellation. Reveal duration/stagger tokens use milliseconds and reveal distance uses pixels. Reduced motion disables animations/transitions and press transforms. Preserve the red treatments and native scrolling; do not add animation dependencies or blanket `transition: all`.

Visible motion coverage: landing/catalog course cards enter individually on view through separate entrance wrappers, so hover transforms remain independent. Cards lift 8px and gently zoom clipped artwork on fine-pointer hover; main/secondary actions lift 4px and arrow icons travel 6px. Surface/hover/FAQ movement uses 500ms and reveal movement uses 500ms/32px. Color changes use the shared 300ms control duration and click press feedback remains 120ms. Learning steps, coaching and FAQ blocks also reveal on view. Card scopes own their entrances to prevent duplicate animation by the surrounding page scope. Touch browsing avoids sticky hover effects, and reduced motion disables these transforms. All measurements remain theme tokens.


## Reusable contracts and ownership

Read the parent [design system](../DESIGN_SYSTEM.md) for canonical font, primitive, export and motion ownership. Add LMS-only tokens to `styles/theme.css`; never copy the shared base. Spacing and radius roles are independent; footer measure is independent of testimonial measure. Small readable accents and compact-action labels are intentionally separate contrast roles from primary red.

`features/marketing/landing.tsx` creates the approved default LandingModel. `components/marketing/landing-page.tsx` accepts supplied per-section props, ordering, optional header/footer and motion policy. Sections can be rendered independently with explicit markers and alternate identifiers. Content imports belong to feature/default composition; renderers receive narrow typed copy/models.

`config/navigation.ts` owns routes, API destinations and section IDs. `config/catalog.ts` owns categories/artwork kinds. Catalog selectors own featured identity/order, numeric duration summaries and validation. Lesson resources follow explicit associations; missing preview is an optional empty state. Learning selectors and sanitation are pure; cookie I/O remains in the server adapter. `config/persistence.ts` owns cookie version/lifetime and learner/course/lesson note scopes. Profile validation is shared by form, API and sanitation.

CourseGrid receives resolved card items, layout and explicit motion policy in public, dashboard and recommendation contexts. Entrance wrapper and hover article are distinct. ContinueCard, DashboardStats, LessonOutline, ResourceCard and CertificateCard receive narrow models rather than full learning state. Repeated controls use unique React IDs. Actions distinguish navigation from mutation; mutation state rejects duplicate submissions and announces errors.

Certificate rendering receives a model and concrete presentation, escapes XML and preserves intrinsic SVG coordinates. The route owns input/eligibility/headers only. Identity facts are shared with the screen card. Demo pricing, cookie state and previews remain demonstrations, not authentication, payment or protected delivery.

Resolve CSS timing units explicitly: optimized `1s` is 1000ms, not 1ms. Surface movement is 500ms, entrance is 500ms, stagger 80ms, lift 8px, action lift 4px and arrow travel 6px. Movement hover applies only to fine pointers. Reduced motion disables nonessential transformations. Closed LMS FAQ panels have zero content height at every supported width; legacy workshop answer padding remains its own variant.


## Shared creator media and testimonial sections

The landing hero uses the supplied workshop intro MP4 and local YouTube thumbnail from `../config/marketing-intro.ts`. A responsive full-width wrapper prevents it collapsing inside the centered hero flex layout. Other consumers can omit `hero.video` to retain the configurable course preview.

`../config/marketing-proof.ts` owns common work-sample and testimonial content; `../config/portfolio.ts` selects the four featured originals and retains all six records. Media mapping preserves original indexes. Shared `@khatab/design/ui/clients-section`, `hero-video`, `testimonials-section`, `review-list`, and supporting cards/identity/marquee components are consumed by both apps. Root workshop component files are import adapters. LandingModel makes work samples/testimonials optional and independently orderable; `reveal` connects them to the existing page scope.

Use one wide featured testimonial plus three smaller reviews, followed by the centered primary-red `عرض المزيد` control with an underline on hover. Expansion reveals the remaining reviews with the shared fade duration. No duration badge appears on work-sample cards.

The shared player starts inline and exposes no provider/native controls or download action. Video clicks pause; completion restores its poster. A new activation resets the previous player, and scrolling it out of view releases its source. Fullscreen remains a deliberate top-right toggle, shown on hover/keyboard focus (always available on touch), with a pointer cursor, white icon and compact black shadow, transparent background and no border. Fullscreen preserves original orientation without cropping; exit control and Escape return inline.

`scripts/sync-marketing-assets.cjs` copies the supplied intro, original portfolio exports, thumbnails and creator portraits into LMS public assets during predev/prebuild. Native playback needs no third-party library. Both 3040 and 3042 serve the same Ta3llam application; source/video access remains public demo delivery.
